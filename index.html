More Than a Teacher. A Conceptual Educator.

Professor Sir Shoaib Hashmat is an English professor at Government Graduate College, Muzaffargarh, with more than two decades of teaching experience. He has taught more than 5,000 students. He does not believe English should be reduced to memorizing grammar rules. His distinctive strength is conceptual teaching: explaining English beyond textbook memorization, so students understand its logic, meaning and practical use.

  • Why is this tense used here?
  • Why is the sentence built this way?
  • How do words work in real communication?
  • How does grammar connect to meaning?
  • How does spoken English differ from textbook English?
  • How can English be used naturally every day?

Questions About Professor Shoaib Hashmat.

Who is Professor Sir Shoaib Hashmat?

Professor Sir Shoaib Hashmat is an English professor and English language educator at Government Graduate College, Muzaffargarh, Punjab, Pakistan. He has more than 20 years of teaching experience and has taught more than 5,000 students.

Where does Professor Shoaib Hashmat teach?

He is an English professor at Government Graduate College, Muzaffargarh, in Punjab, Pakistan.

What does Professor Shoaib Hashmat teach?

He teaches English grammar, spoken English and English communication skills, including tenses, parts of speech, sentence structure, subject-verb agreement, active and passive voice, direct and indirect speech, pronunciation and vocabulary.

Does Professor Shoaib Hashmat teach spoken English?

Yes. Spoken English is taught as a practical skill: everyday conversation, greetings and introductions, fluency, pronunciation, vocabulary, confidence, and classroom and professional communication.

Does Professor Shoaib Hashmat teach English grammar?

Yes. English grammar is explained conceptually through topics such as tenses, parts of speech, active and passive voice, direct and indirect speech, subject-verb agreement and sentence structure. His English tense lectures are available on YouTube.

What is conceptual English teaching?

Conceptual English teaching explains English beyond textbook memorization, so students understand its logic, meaning and practical use. In his approach, a concept becomes a skill in five steps: concept, explanation, example, practice and communication.

Where is Professor Shoaib Hashmat based?

He works in Muzaffargarh, Punjab, Pakistan, as an English professor at Government Graduate College, Muzaffargarh.
